Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about estimates, pricing, and creatives.

PriceMyProject is designed to give users a clearer starting point before hiring creative talent. These answers explain how estimates work and what they should—and should not—be used for.

PriceMyProject estimates are based on publicly available pricing information, published freelance and studio rate ranges, project scope, complexity, turnaround time, revision count, usage rights, and other factors that commonly affect creative pricing. The goal is to provide market-informed guidance, not a guaranteed quote.

The biggest factors are usually project type, scope, complexity, quality level, deadline, revision count, and usage rights. For some categories, duration, page count, asset count, or team size can also significantly change the estimate.
Usage rights define how the finished work can be used. Personal use, organic social posts, commercial use, paid advertising, resale, or perpetual worldwide rights can have very different values. Broader rights often increase the price because the work is being used more extensively.
Revisions require additional labor and production time. A project with one small revision round is very different from a project with unlimited changes, so the revision allowance can meaningfully affect pricing.
Faster turnaround can require a creator to rearrange their schedule, work longer hours, delay other projects, or add extra team members. Rush work may therefore carry a higher price.
